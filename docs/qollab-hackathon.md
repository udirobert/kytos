## Provenance and what ran

The pre-hackathon Cleveland work constructed protein contact graphs, computed active-site-conditioned CTQW and classical random-walk comparisons, packaged ideal Qiskit evolutions, and later ran Moth `graph-v1` coupling-map tomography on IBM `ibm_fez`. The Moth protocol is **not** the Qollab walk circuit; its hardware results do not validate this new circuit's biological predictions. See [`docs/cleveland/method-report.md`](cleveland/method-report.md), [`c008` report](../experiments/cleveland/c008-moth-qpu-tomography/narrative/report.md), and the [quantum exhibit](https://kytosapp.netlify.app/quantum/).

The Qollab addition embeds a disclosed, induced four-supernode slice of the **previously recorded** 20-node KRAS core ([source graph](../experiments/cleveland/c008-moth-qpu-tomography/metrics/graphs/kras_core.json)): original nodes `[0, 3, 11, 8]`, source node 0, known-pocket readout node 8, edges `[(0,1),(0,2),(1,2),(2,3)]` in local indices. The control deletes local edge `(0,2)`. This slice and readout were selected with knowledge of the graph and pocket; the highlighted time `t=2.0` is **illustrative, not pre-registered or blind**. It is not a full-protein site-ranking result.

Current code sends **one four-qubit circuit** containing two independent two-qubit Laplacian walks at `t=2.0` in one job, avoiding the multi-circuit job format rejected by Qollab's IonQ endpoint. A five-time table is computed exactly/classically and labeled a **reference sweep, not measurements**. The reference classical walker is `Q = D^-1 A - I` and the quantum walk uses `L = D - A`. Six local targeted tests and Ruff passed. An earlier 10-circuit variant succeeded on Qollab's free built-in simulator; the current single-circuit version passed locally on Qiskit's BasicSimulator and in a user-observed Qollab run on the **free IonQ Forte 1 remote noise-model simulator** (not physical IonQ hardware). On the IonQ run at 2,048 shots, recorded/altered readout probabilities at `t=2.0` were **0.19141 / 0.39551** versus exact ideal references **0.19051 / 0.40502**; corresponding classical references were **0.07991 / 0.05758**. Those numbers are a *single illustrative run*, not an estimate of predictive accuracy, noise advantage, or statistical significance. The initial one-qubit VQE toy was retired; it was not molecular chemistry.

## Landscape and research gap

- [Mohtashim et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17486v1) already studied CTQW centrality across about 150 protein residue networks and executed a proof-of-principle IBM hardware example. Its global centrality walk starts uniformly; it is not evidence that our localized-source method predicts a distal pocket. Do **not** claim first CTQW on proteins or first quantum hardware protein graph.
- [Amor et al. (2016)](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms12477) previously used *classical*, active-site-conditioned bond-to-bond graph propagation for allostery. Do **not** claim first source-conditioned allostery graph method.
- [AlloBench (2025)](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10594075-allobench-data-set-pipeline-development-benchmarking-allosteric-site-prediction-tools) offers active/allosteric-site annotations for a future broader benchmark. [AlloDyn (2026)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.22.727284v1) warns that certain holo-structure evaluations with bound modulators leak labels. Use apo inputs and hold pocket labels out of selection when claiming prediction.
- Our existing [`c004`](../experiments/cleveland/c004-randomization/narrative/report.md) and [`c007`](../experiments/cleveland/c007-topology-levers/narrative/report.md) site-ranking variants did **not** clear their edge-rewire significance checks. `c008` did not establish site recovery; its role-level hardware correlations were inconsistent across targets. The current Qollab four-node contrast does not overturn these limitations.

**Proposed contribution, not yet an outcome:** Test whether *localized active-site CTQW* adds information about held-out distal pockets beyond classical diffusion, graph distance/degree, and matched rewired or decoy-pocket controls. Freeze graph construction, source definitions, time aggregation, and readouts **before** examining held-out labels. Evaluate all eligible apo proteins in a declared panel, report uncertainty and failures, and do not label a visually pleasing edge-deletion response 'biological causality'. A negative result with a reusable falsification harness is still a contribution; it is not quantum advantage. The present Qollab code is a runnable explanatory slice, **not** that benchmark. A larger AlloBench evaluation is future work, not a completed submission result.
