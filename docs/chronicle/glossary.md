## Voice rules

- Define with a question or a kitchen metaphor; never jargon-on-jargon.
- An entry may be quoted/re-shared standalone — it must survive without its
  source short.
- New entry = one line here + one line on the introducing short's card.

## Planned next (order of appearance)

  up/down gene set right?

- Pearson Δ — the correlation-with-logs measure; a constant predictor has
