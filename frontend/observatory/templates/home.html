Why the score is not enough 02 / 03

Looks right. Is wrong.

  1. Models that score well can still be biologically wrong. A good number is not a working prediction.
  2. Most evals only check whether the output looks right — not whether it is right.
  3. In drug prediction, “looks right but is wrong” is a drug that doesn’t work, discovered a year too late.

The score tells you how close the answer is. The cracks tell you where it’s biologically wrong — and only the cracks can stop a bad prediction before it reaches the lab.
